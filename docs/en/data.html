Data

This page. The values behind the charts and tables, as 28 CSV files to download.

All tables are UTF-8 CSV. No running text is included. In the Jeonyul tongbo segment table, kyujanggak_image is the page image number of the Kyujanggak archive (volume_leaf + side). The terms of the sources are on the Method page.

FileContents
compilations.csvEight compilations (the seven intermediate ones and the Yukjeon jorye, dated by its preface, 1866): year and basis, jomok, provisions (unit), characters of the six jeon, penal share, dated strings and range
dated_instructions_by_reign.csvDated instruction strings by compilation and reign
dated_instructions_by_decade.csvThe same by compilation and decade
jeonyul_tongbo_marks.csvJeonyul tongbo: share of characters by mark, five readings/allocations
jeonyul_tongbo_marks_by_jeon.csvJeonyul tongbo: marks by jeon
sillok_code_mentions.csvVeritable Records: articles naming each code, by reign and edition (principal = 1 is the main count)
sinbo_sugyo_reign_share.csvSinbo sugyo jimnok: share of dated strings by reign, with the values quoted by Kim Baek-cheol 2009 (p. 226)
reigns.csvReign table: first regnal year, from the Sillok headings
jeonyul_tongbo_segments.csvJeonyul tongbo segments: ID, jeon and jomok, marks, characters, Kyujanggak page (no text)
daejeon_hoetong_marks_by_jeon.csvDaejeon hoetong: marks (原, 續, 增, 補) by jeon, counts and shares
daejeon_hoetong_layers_by_jeon.csvDaejeon hoetong: share of characters by layer and jeon — main (office tables left out), with tables, strict reading
daejeon_hoetong_first_layer.csvDaejeon hoetong: jomok by the layer in which they first appear, per jeon
daejeon_hoetong_first_layer_not_won.csvThe 16 jomok not beginning in 原: NIKH item ID, title, jeon, layer (no text)
late_codes_in_sillok_by_year.csvGojong and Sunjong Veritable Records: articles per year and articles naming 大典會通, 六典條例, 刑法大全
gyeongguk_daejeon_provisions_1785_1865.csvGyeongguk daejeon provisions in 1785 and 1865 by category and jeon, with the 1706 reprint baseline and the collation-note sensitivity
daejeon_hoetong_marks_vs_sources.csvDaejeon hoetong (and tongpyeon): characters under each mark, found in which earlier code — three readings, by jeon
four_codes_size.csvThe four codes: characters and provisions by jeon (1746 = both books)
daejeon_hoetong_rebuilt_by_layer.csvThe Daejeon hoetong rebuilt layer by layer (原, 原續, 原續增, all)
alignment_coverage.csvCoverage of each pair of codes (thresholds 4/8 and 6/12, reprint baseline included)
abolished_marks.csv今廢 and 今革 by book and jeon (totals only)
jomok_kept_new_gone.csvJomok titles kept, new and gone, by jeon
penal_terms.csvWords for punishments in the Penal Codes, counts and per 10,000 characters
code_references.csvWords naming a code, by citing book and layer
alignment_note_excluded.csvThe 156 Gyeongguk daejeon provisions left out of the collation-note sensitivity: NIKH id of the last TOC item where the provision starts, start in the normalized Han text of that item, length (one provision runs on into the next item), category in 1865
alignment_blind_agreement.csvBlind agreement (provision categories, crossed stretches, the 今廢 split)
yukjeon_jorye_by_jeon.csvYukjeon jorye by jeon: offices, leaf items, characters (main text, notes), ○ marks
hyeongbeop_daejeon_parts.csvHyeongbeop daejeon by part: chapters, sections, articles, characters (counted = 1 is the code itself)
dated_instructions.csvList of dated strings: compilation, node ID, string, year and how it was set
